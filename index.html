FORSAKEN

Forsaken is a asymetrical horror game made by Hytoko and Basil in the end of 2024.

Survivors

Survivors is what you mostly play as in Forsaken, the survivors get divided into 3 groups which is Sentinels, Supports and Survivalists.

Killers

You play as a killer once your malice is the highest of the server, who would you be? Noli, C00lkidd, John Doe, 1x1x1x1 or Slasher?

Two Time

Two Time is one of the survivors in Forsaken, he is a sneaky sentinel with a mix of survivalist in his kit.

1x1x1x1

1x1x1x1 is a ranged killer able to pressure the survivors with his minions and ranged abilities.
